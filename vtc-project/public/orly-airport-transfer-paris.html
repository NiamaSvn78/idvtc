Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Orly from central Paris?

Orly Airport is approximately 14 km south of central Paris. The transfer takes 20–40 minutes depending on traffic. Much closer than CDG — and often quicker even in peak hours.

Which terminal does my driver wait in?

Orly has 4 terminals: Orly 1 (international), Orly 2 (domestic & EU), Orly 3, and Orly 4. Your driver waits in the arrivals hall of your specific terminal with a name sign. The meeting point is confirmed in your booking email.

What if my connecting flight is late and I miss my slot?

We track your inbound flight in real time. If your connection is delayed, your driver adjusts — no rebooking needed. Contact us on WhatsApp for any cancellations.

Can I go from Paris to Orly (return transfer)?

Yes. Book both directions at once for a seamless trip. For the return, your driver picks you up at your Paris address and drives directly to your departure terminal.

Are there any extra fees I should know about?

None. The fixed fare at booking covers everything — tolls, airport fees, fuel, child seats on request. If your wait exceeds 60 minutes after landing, a small hourly fee applies, visible in the booking summary.
