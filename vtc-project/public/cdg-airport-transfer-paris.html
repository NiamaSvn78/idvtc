A CDG airport transfer priced before you land

Charles de Gaulle sits 25 km north-east of central Paris, and the ride into town crosses the A1 motorway and the Boulevard Périphérique, two of the busiest roads in France. With IsmaDrive, that journey costs a flat €100 between any Paris address (postcodes 75001 to 75020) and any CDG terminal, in either direction. The amount is locked in when you book: a jam at the Porte de la Chapelle or a 6 am pick-up does not move it by a single euro.

Four real journeys from Roissy-Charles de Gaulle

CDG Terminal 2E → Gare de Lyon (Paris 12th): €100

Long-haul arrivals into 2E often connect with a TGV heading to Lyon, Marseille or the Alps. Your chauffeur drops you at the station after roughly 45 to 60 minutes, leaving time to find your platform without dragging suitcases through the RER B.

Opéra Garnier hotels (Paris 9th) → CDG Terminal 1: €100

Leaving from the Grands Boulevards for a Star Alliance flight from the round Terminal 1? Count 40 to 55 minutes outside the morning peak. The fare is the same as from any other arrondissement.

CDG Terminal 2F → Château de Versailles (Yvelines, 78): €100 + €2.50/km beyond Paris

Versailles lies on the far side of the capital, so the drive usually takes 60 to 80 minutes via the A86. Because the château is outside Paris, the €100 airport fare is topped up by €2.50 for each kilometre beyond the first 5 km from the city centre. The booking tool calculates that supplement from your exact address and shows the total before you pay.

CDG Terminal 3 → Disneyland Paris (Marne-la-Vallée, 77): €100 + €2.50/km beyond Paris

Charter and low-cost passengers from Terminal 3 can skip Paris entirely: the A104 ring road takes you to the park hotels in about 35 to 45 minutes. The same rule applies, the €100 airport fare plus the per-kilometre supplement outside the city, displayed in your quote.

What every transfer includes

  • Real-time flight tracking: we follow your flight number, so a delayed landing simply shifts your pick-up time.
  • 60 minutes of free waiting after touchdown, enough for passport control and baggage reclaim.
  • A name sign in the arrivals hall of your terminal, whether it is T1, one of the T2 halls (2A to 2G) or T3.
  • Baby seat and booster seat at no extra charge; mention your children's ages when booking.
  • A Mercedes of your choice: the E-Class saloon for up to 3 passengers, or the V-Class Premium Van for up to 7 passengers with space for pushchairs and extra luggage.
  • WhatsApp contact with the team before and during the ride, plus secure card payment online.

Arriving for a business trip with meetings across the city? Keep the same chauffeur by the hour: €75/h in the E-Class, €95/h in the V-Class, 2 hours minimum.

CDG transfer FAQ

Which terminal will my driver wait at?

The one your flight lands at, shown on the airline's arrivals board. Terminal 2 is split into seven halls, from 2A to 2G, which are several minutes apart, so tell us your hall when you book and your chauffeur will stand at that exact arrivals exit.

Is the €100 fare the same at night and on Sundays?

Yes. Between Paris and Charles de Gaulle the fare stays at €100 at every hour, every day of the year, including red-eye flights landing before dawn.

My hotel is in Saint-Denis, near the Stade de France. What will I pay?

Saint-Denis is in Seine-Saint-Denis (93), outside Paris, so the €100 airport fare is increased by €2.50 for each kilometre beyond 5 km from the city centre. Enter the hotel address in the booking form and the exact price appears before payment.

Can you collect me from the TGV station at Terminal 2?

Yes. The Aéroport Charles de Gaulle 2 TGV station sits between Terminals 2C/2E and 2D/2F. Tell us your train's arrival time when booking, or on WhatsApp, and your driver will meet you as you come up from the platforms.
