Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the drive from CDG to Paris take?

Typically 30–60 minutes to central Paris depending on traffic and your destination. Rush hours (7–9 am, 5–7 pm) may add time. Your driver will always take the fastest route in real time.

Where exactly does my driver wait at CDG?

In the arrivals hall of your terminal — T1, T2A, T2B, T2C, T2D, T2E, T2F, T2G, or T3. Your confirmation email specifies the exact meeting point. Your driver holds a sign with your name.

What if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

We track your flight in real time. Delays are handled automatically — your driver arrives when your flight lands. For cancellations, contact us directly on WhatsApp: +33 6 23 88 97 17.

Can I book a return transfer (Paris to CDG)?

Yes. Book your outbound and return transfers at the same time. For the return, we pick you up at your Paris address and drive you directly to your departure terminal.

Is the price really fixed? No hidden fees?

Completely fixed. Tolls, fuel, airport parking — all included. What you see at booking is exactly what you pay. We never add traffic or surge fees.
